\section{Conclusion}

This paper presented TrustAuditFlow, a lightweight extension of the BLoP full-node trusted audit model for blockchain-based distributed data verification in data circulation. The design keeps BLoP as the high-assurance baseline and adds a risk-aware committee path for routine audit events. It separates off-chain verification and recovery from on-chain anchoring, uses Merkle roots and hash-chain states for traceability, connects anomaly detection with erasure-code-assisted recovery, and updates node trust scores after audit outcomes. TrustAuditFlow provides a structured audit workflow that combines off-chain verification, on-chain accountability, recovery closure, and full-node fallback for data circulation scenarios.
