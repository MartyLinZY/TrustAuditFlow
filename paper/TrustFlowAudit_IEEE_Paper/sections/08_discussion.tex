\section{Discussion and Future Work}

TrustAuditFlow focuses on audit-flow orchestration for data circulation. Its value is in clarifying how a BLoP-style full-node trusted audit system can support a lighter path for routine events without discarding the full-node path. The design also connects components that are often discussed separately: PDP/PoR-style integrity checking, blockchain anchoring, erasure-code recovery, and trust-based node governance.

The design has several scope boundaries. First, the trust score abstracts BLoP's trusted measurement idea and can be bound to TPM, TEE, SGX, or remote-attestation evidence in concrete deployments. Second, the verification interface supports hash checking, PDP/PoR, or zero-knowledge proof modules depending on the required assurance level. Third, the PBFT-style confirmation path can be instantiated on consortium-chain platforms such as FISCO BCOS, Fabric, Tendermint, or related systems. Fourth, the paper focuses on block-level and evidence-level integrity rather than the semantic correctness of credit features, privacy-preserving queries, or federated-learning updates.

Future work includes binding trust scores to concrete remote-attestation evidence, integrating public PDP/PoR or zero-knowledge verification modules, and connecting the audit workflow to consortium-chain and privacy-preserving computation frameworks so that task logs and result hashes from data circulation workflows can be audited end to end.
